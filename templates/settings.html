Privacy and safety

Access requires a signed-in account and patient data is not exposed publicly. This prototype uses simulated patient data and is intended for demonstration and educational purposes. CareNest AI is an AI-assisted early risk detection and caregiver decision-support system; it does not diagnose, and does not replace doctors, nurses or emergency services.

Privacy by design: the system collects only vital signs, equipment status, caregiver notes and check-in times. There is no camera, microphone or location tracking, no third-party analytics, and all data stays in the local SQLite database. Caregiver attendance uses a simulated bedside tap, not tracking of the caregiver.
